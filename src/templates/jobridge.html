How it works

              

            


            

              
Which forecasting model, and why?

              

              

              

              

                Rolling backtest: at each forecast origin every 3 months, each model sees only the data up to that month and forecasts 6 and 12 months ahead; forecasts are compared with what happened. Selection rule, fixed in advance: among models that respond to the AI scenario, the lowest error at 12 months.
